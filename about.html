    DIFFERENT TYPES OF SOILS AND GROLDEN CROPS FOR SOILS

    *Characteristics of different types of soil and soils golden crops

    1.Black soil: Good for cotton, sugarcane, tobacco, millets, wheat, and oilseeds

    2.Alluvial soil: Good for rice, wheat, sugarcane, and cotton

    3.Red and yellow soil: Good for maize, groundnut, rice, fruits like mango, orange, vegetables, potato, and pulses

    4.Arid soil: Good for bajra, pulses, guar, fodder, and less water requiring crops

    5.Forest soil: Rich in humus but lacks potash, lime, and phosphorus

    6.Mountainous soil: Good for orchard crops 

    7.Clay soil: Heavy and compact structure that holds moisture well

    8.Laterite soil: Good for cashew nuts.

PESTICIDES FOR CROP

pesticide Usage:

* Purpose:

* Control pests (insects, weeds, fungi, rodents) that damage crops.

* Protect stored agricultural products.

* Prevent the spread of diseases carried by pests.

* Types:

* Insecticides: Target insects.

* Herbicides: Target weeds.

* Fungicides: Target fungi.

* Rodenticides: Target rodents.

* Application Methods:

* Spraying (aerial, ground).

* Dusting.

* Seed treatments.

* Soil incorporation.

Fertilizer Usage:

* Purpose:

* Provide essential nutrients (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium) for plant growth.

* Improve soil fertility.

* Increase crop yields.

* Types:

* Synthetic (chemical) fertilizers.

* Organic fertilizers (manure, compost, green manure).

* Application Methods:

* Broadcasting.

* Banding.

* Fertigation (application through irrigation).

* Side dressing.
